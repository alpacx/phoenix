Phoenix / RK3562 — package, LPDDR4 and startup design review
Supplier enquiry draft, 24 November 2026

We are designing a passively cooled USB-C network gateway around the Rockchip
RK3562 and RK809-5. The board uses one SK hynix H9HCNNN8KUMLHR-NME
8 Gbit LPDDR4 device with two x16 channels and one chip select per channel.
VDD1 is 1.8 V. VDD2 and VDDQ are 1.1 V. Storage is Samsung KLM8G1GETF-B041
eMMC. We would appreciate revision-controlled guidance for the following items.

1. Please provide the RK3562 hardware design guide, qualified LPDDR4 layout
   and associated PCB stackup. For the FCCSP478L, please specify the permitted
   PCB copper-land diameter and tolerance, solder-mask opening diameter and
   registration, stencil aperture and thickness, and via-in-pad finish.
2. On the 0.50 mm pitch array our current 0.250 mm copper lands and 1:1
   mask openings leave 0.250 mm between adjacent openings. A 0.0762 mm
   trace with 0.090 mm mask clearance on each side needs 0.2562 mm. Please
   identify a qualified fanout for these crossings and provide the assembly
   tolerance data for any alternative opening or land pattern.
3. Please confirm PHY and DRAM calibration circuitry for this part and
   package. Our schematic follows the RK3562 EVB2 V10 LPDDR4 reference for
   the 120 ohm PHY RZQ pull-up on ball 1D4. The DRAM uses its own 240 ohm
   ZQ resistor to VDDQ. Please confirm resistor tolerances, placement and
   the required treatment of ball 1C9 in LPDDR4 mode.
4. Please provide target impedances, allowed topology, package delays, byte
   lane and CA/clock skew limits, via models, ODT settings, and validated
   rates with this exact LPDDR4 part. Please identify any vendor training
   firmware, version, required device configuration, and redistribution terms.
5. Please confirm power sequencing and ramp limits, RK809-5 rail defaults,
   reset timing and the required treatment of unused CSI/DSI/OTP domains.
   Please provide SoC current transients or target impedances by rail and
   recommended placement and connections of package decouplers.

The custom board is in CAD routing. Measurements above are nominal CAD
dimensions. Electrical, assembly and thermal validation on a prototype
remains pending.
